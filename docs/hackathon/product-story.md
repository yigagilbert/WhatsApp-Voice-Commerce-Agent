# Product story

## The problem

Across East Africa and many emerging markets, small businesses sell through **conversations** —
WhatsApp chats, voice notes, phone calls — not e-commerce storefronts. A craft seller in Kampala
takes an order by voice note, works out totals by hand, sends bank details or a payment link
manually, then scrolls back through chats to remember who paid. Bookkeeping lives in their head.

Existing tools assume a storefront, a laptop, English, and typing.

## The idea

A commerce assistant that lives where the merchant already works. The merchant says:

> "Create an order for Sarah for 50 handmade baskets at $12 each and add $80 shipping."

…and gets back a precise order summary, confirms with one reply, and a PayPal payment link goes
out. When Sarah pays, the merchant hears about it on WhatsApp, and the dashboard shows revenue,
outstanding payments and the full activity trail. Later: "How much have I sold this week?" — an
accurate answer from real data.

## Who it's for

- Micro and small merchants selling via WhatsApp/social (crafts, food, fashion, agriculture).
- Merchants more comfortable speaking than typing — and in their own language (Luganda first).
- Cross-border sellers who need to accept international payments (PayPal).

## Why it's trustworthy

Money is where AI mistakes hurt most, so the design makes the AI **advisory**:

- The AI interprets; deterministic code calculates; the merchant confirms; only then does PayPal execute.
- Amounts the merchant sees are generated by the backend, not the model.
- Every AI decision and financial event is audited and traceable by correlation ID.

## Why now

- Voice and multilingual ASR (e.g. Sunbird AI for Ugandan languages) have become good enough.
- LLM tool-calling makes it possible to map messy speech to structured commands reliably — when
  paired with validation.
- PayPal provides hosted checkout, invoicing and webhooks merchants can trust without building payments.

## Differentiators

1. **Voice-first and multilingual** by design, not as an add-on.
2. **Channel-agnostic core** — WhatsApp today; web chat, Telegram, mobile and voice tomorrow.
3. **Responsible AI for money** — confirmation-bound actions, deterministic totals, audit trail.
4. **Merchant dashboard** that turns conversations into business records.

## Roadmap highlights

- Luganda ASR/translation via Sunbird AI providers; spoken (TTS) replies.
- PayPal invoicing, refunds and subscriptions behind the same confirmation mechanism.
- Official WhatsApp Cloud API; additional channels.
- AG Grid-powered data views; AI-generated business insights.
- Multi-merchant onboarding with proper authentication.
